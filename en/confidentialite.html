Privacy and personal data

Demonstration mock-up

This site is a mock-up created by OBT Agency for the Domaine des Chabriers. This page sets out what the final website's privacy policy will contain; it will be approved by the estate before going live.

Who is responsible for your data

SARL Domaine des Chabriers, 470 route de la Salette, Les Chabriers, 26120 Ourches, represented by Céline Duverney, manager. For any question about your data: domainedeschabriers@gmail.com.

The enquiry form

The form on the contact page stores nothing on the website: it prepares an email in your own mail app, which you choose to send or not. The information you send us (name, contact details, dates, number of people, message) is used only to answer your request and to prepare your stay or event. Only Céline Duverney, manager, reads it. It is kept for 2 years after our last exchange, then deleted. It is never sold or passed on.

The calendar

The availability calendar records no data: the dates you choose are simply carried over into the enquiry form.

Cookies and third-party services

The site sets no audience measurement or advertising cookies. The map on the contact page is provided by Google Maps: when it is displayed, Google may receive your IP address and set its own cookies, in line with its privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Hosting

The mock-up is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may keep technical connection logs. The host of the final website will be specified when it goes live.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, its correction or deletion, or object to its use, by writing to domainedeschabriers@gmail.com. If you feel your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr).
