Iyengar Yoga retreat & active walking

Cleanse the body, find your breath again and leave full of vitality. From Thursday 6 to Sunday 9 May 2027.

Book my retreat The brochure (PDF)

Drôme · Vercors · Spring 2027 · Springtime

Spring is the favourite season for cleansing the body of toxins and regenerating.

Food, breathing, movement, thoughts and emotions all play their part in this balance.

These 4 days will be a chance to put these principles into practice and leave full of vitality.

An invitation to vitality

Four days of positive energy for yourself, in the heart of nature, between the Drôme valley and the foothills of the Vercors. This retreat combines the rigorous practice of Iyengar Yoga with active walking in the mountains.

Mornings open gently and gradually, ensuring thegrounding of the structure that holds us. The Iyengar method is solid: it calls for precision of movement, our attention and our liveliness.

The days continue on the trails, at the rhythm of the breath, with picnics facing the landscapes.

The practice of Iyengar Yoga

Each morning, we will explore the method created by BKS Iyengar, renowned worldwide for its rigour and intelligence. This generous, demanding and precise practice develops a deep awareness of the body.

We will progress through several families of postures : standing poses for grounding and vitality, backbends for opening the heart, forward bends and seated poses for introspection, inversions for a new perspective.

“If you are balanced in the present, you live in eternity. Yoga teaches you to live in the present.”

BKS Iyengar

Discovery & improvement

Who is this retreat for?

Everyone is welcome who has at least a year of yoga practice and/or good body awareness, and who enjoys walking. No performance is required, just curiosity and the wish to reconnect with yourself.

Lydia will guide you with kindness and expertise. Equipment (blocks, straps, blankets) is provided.

Accommodation limited to 14 people, for personal guidance and a friendly atmosphere.

The benefits of the practice

  • RelaxationConcentration and relaxation grow; inner awareness becomes finer.
  • StrengthStrength, endurance and body alignment, as well as flexibility.
  • VitalityPowerful legs, better circulation, coordination and balance.
  • HealthMany students find their physical difficulties ease.
  • MeditationThe mind unites with body and breath, reaching a meditative state.
  • BreathActive walking extends the morning's work: deep breathing, a free mind.

Active walking

On the trails of the Drôme and the Vercors

Each day, a 4 to 5 hour walk with a picnic, depending on the weather. Two outings are led by Céline and Lydia; on your return, an hour of relaxation and unwinding.

Teachers

Your guides

  • Lydia Boukhirane

    Iyengar Yoga teacher

    An artist researching in the field for 30 years, Lydia combines her artistic practice with teaching Iyengar Yoga. Trained at the Centre Victor Hugo in Paris from 2022, she earned her teaching certificate in 2025.

    Estate manager

    Keeper of the Domaine des Chabriers and a lover of art and nature, Céline welcomes you, shares her intimate knowledge of the Drôme and leads the walks with Lydia.
